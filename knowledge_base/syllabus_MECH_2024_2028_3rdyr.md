# Department of Mechanical Engineering (MECH)
## Curriculum & Syllabus - Year: 3rd Year | Batch: 2024-2028
**Curriculum Version:** v1.0 (AY2024-25 onwards)
**Total Degree Minimum Credits:** 160 Credits
**Year Focus:** 3rd Year Core & Professional Foundations

### Mandatory Core Subjects for 3rd Year MECH:
- **MEC3001: Kinematics and Dynamics of Machinery** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **MEC3004: Heat and Mass Transfer** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **MEC3007: Design of Machine Elements & Transmission Systems** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **MEC3011: CAD/CAM/CAE and Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** | Credits: 3 | Status: Mandatory Core

### Specialization Elective Baskets Available for MECH:
Students must earn specialized elective credits from the following official departmental baskets:
- Thermal & Energy Engineering (Refrigeration & AC, Power Plant Engineering, Electric Vehicle Thermal Management)
- Automotive Engineering (Vehicle Dynamics, Hybrid Powertrain, Chassis & Suspension Design)
- Robotics & Automation (Hydraulics & Pneumatics, PLC Programming, Industrial Automation)
- Smart Manufacturing & 3D Printing (Additive Manufacturing, CNC Machining, Industry 4.0)

### Degree Completion Credit Breakdown (2024-2028 Scheme):
- School Core Basket: 58 credits
- Program Core Basket: 42 credits
- Discipline & Specialized Electives: 42 credits
- Open Electives: 18 credits
- Total Minimum Credits to Graduate: 160 credits

### Regulations & Special Instructions:
- Minimum 75% attendance in theory and laboratory classes is strictly required to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
- Non-credit mandatory courses include Environmental Studies (CHE1001) and Co-/Extra-curricular Activities (CEA1001).
- For 2024-2028 batch, Capstone Project Phase-1 begins in the 7th Semester and Capstone Phase-2 / Full-time Internship is in the 8th Semester.
