# Department of Civil Engineering (CIVIL)
## Curriculum & Syllabus - Year: 2nd Year | Batch: 2022-2026
**Curriculum Version:** v2.0 (AY2022-23 standard)
**Total Degree Minimum Credits:** 168 Credits
**Year Focus:** 2nd Year Core & Professional Foundations

### Mandatory Core Subjects for 2nd Year CIVIL:
- **CIV2001: Mechanics of Solids & Deformable Bodies** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **CIV2003: Fluid Mechanics and Open Channel Flow** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **CIV2005: Structural Analysis - I (Indeterminate Structures)** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **CIV2008: Concrete Technology & Building Construction Practice** | Credits: 3 | Status: Mandatory Core

### Specialization Elective Baskets Available for CIVIL:
Students must earn specialized elective credits from the following official departmental baskets:
- Structural Engineering (Earthquake Resistant Design, Steel Structures, Prestressed Concrete)
- Environmental & Water Resources (Water Treatment Plant Design, Urban Hydrology, GIS)
- Smart Infrastructure (BIM - Building Information Modeling, Green Building, Smart Cities)
- Geotechnical & Foundation Engineering (Deep Foundations, Slope Stability, Soil Dynamics)

### Degree Completion Credit Breakdown (2022-2026 Scheme):
- School Core Basket: 62 credits
- Program Core Basket: 46 credits
- Discipline & Specialized Electives: 42 credits
- Open Electives: 18 credits
- Total Minimum Credits to Graduate: 168 credits

### Regulations & Special Instructions:
- Minimum 75% attendance in theory and laboratory classes is strictly required to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
- Non-credit mandatory courses include Environmental Studies (CHE1001) and Co-/Extra-curricular Activities (CEA1001).
- For 2022-2026 batch, Capstone Project Phase-1 begins in the 7th Semester and Capstone Phase-2 / Full-time Internship is in the 8th Semester.
