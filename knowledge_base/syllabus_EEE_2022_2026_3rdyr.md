# Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
## Curriculum & Syllabus - Year: 3rd Year | Batch: 2022-2026
**Curriculum Version:** v2.0 (AY2022-23 standard)
**Total Degree Minimum Credits:** 168 Credits
**Year Focus:** 3rd Year Core & Professional Foundations

### Mandatory Core Subjects for 3rd Year EEE:
- **EEE3001: Power System Analysis and Operation** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **EEE3003: Power Electronics and Drives** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **EEE3005: Control Systems Engineering & State Variable Analysis** | Credits: 4 | Status: Mandatory Core
- **EEE3008: Microcontroller Systems for Power Conversion** | Credits: 3 | Status: Mandatory Core

### Specialization Elective Baskets Available for EEE:
Students must earn specialized elective credits from the following official departmental baskets:
- Renewable Energy Systems (Solar PV Engineering, Wind Turbine Systems, Grid Integration)
- Electric Vehicle Systems (Battery Management Systems, EV Motor Drives, Fast Charging Infrastructure)
- Smart Grids & Automation (SCADA, Energy Internet, Microgrid Protection)
- High Voltage Engineering (Insulation Technology, Overvoltage Transients, Switchgear)

### Degree Completion Credit Breakdown (2022-2026 Scheme):
- School Core Basket: 62 credits
- Program Core Basket: 46 credits
- Discipline & Specialized Electives: 42 credits
- Open Electives: 18 credits
- Total Minimum Credits to Graduate: 168 credits

### Regulations & Special Instructions:
- Minimum 75% attendance in theory and laboratory classes is strictly required to be eligible for end-semester examinations.
- Non-credit mandatory courses include Environmental Studies (CHE1001) and Co-/Extra-curricular Activities (CEA1001).
- For 2022-2026 batch, Capstone Project Phase-1 begins in the 7th Semester and Capstone Phase-2 / Full-time Internship is in the 8th Semester.
